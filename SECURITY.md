# Security Policy
# Purpose
    
This Security Policy outlines the measures to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the proprietary software and its associated documentation files (the "Software"). The policy aims to prevent unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of the Software.

# Scope
This policy applies to all individuals who have access to the Software, including employees, contractors, vendors, and any other third-party entities.

# Security Objectives
Confidentiality: Protect the Software from unauthorized access, disclosure, or use.
Integrity: Ensure the Software is not modified, altered, or destroyed without authorization.
Availability: Ensure the Software is accessible and usable by authorized individuals.

# Security Measures
Access Control: Access to the Software will be restricted to authorized individuals who have a legitimate need to access the Software.
Authentication: Authorized individuals will be required to authenticate themselves before accessing the Software.
Encryption: The Software will be encrypted to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.
Firewalls: Firewalls will be used to restrict access to the Software from unauthorized networks or systems.
Monitoring: The Software will be monitored for suspicious activity, and incidents will be reported and investigated.
Physical Security: The Software will be stored in a secure physical location, with access restricted to authorized individuals.

# Incident Response
In the event of a security incident, the following procedures will be followed:
Incident Reporting: Incidents will be reported to the designated security officer.
Incident Assessment: The incident will be assessed to determine the scope and impact.
Incident Containment: Measures will be taken to contain the incident and prevent further damage.
Incident Eradication: Measures will be taken to eradicate the root cause of the incident.
Incident Recovery: Measures will be taken to recover from the incident and restore normal operations.
Compliance
This Security Policy will be reviewed and updated annually, or as needed. All individuals with access to the Software will be required to acknowledge and comply with this policy.
Consequences of Non-Compliance
Failure to comply with this Security Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Additionally, individuals who intentionally or negligently compromise the security of the Software may be subject to civil or criminal liability.
# Acknowledgement
By accessing or attempting to access the Software, individuals acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to comply with this Security Policy.
